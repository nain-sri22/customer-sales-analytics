# Customer & Sales Analytics Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer and sales data using Python to identify sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and key business insights.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, correlation analysis, and business insight generation.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains 100 sales transactions with information about:

- Order ID
- Customer ID
- Date
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Sales
- City

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Checked missing values
- Checked duplicate records
- Converted date values into datetime format
- Verified dataset structure and data types

### 2. Business KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value

### 3. Product Analysis
- Sales by product
- Quantity sold by product
- Average product price
- Top-performing products

### 4. Customer Analysis
- Top customers by sales
- Customers by number of orders
- Average customer spending
- Customer segmentation

### 5. Time-Based Analysis
- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly order trends
- Monthly average order value

### 6. City Analysis
- Sales by city
- Orders by city
- Average order value by city
- Top product by city

### 7. Correlation Analysis
Analyzed relationships between:
- Price and Quantity
- Price and Sales
- Quantity and Sales

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Laptops generated the highest revenue at ₹21.7 lakh.
- Headphones recorded the highest sales volume with 74 units.
- Customer 1016 generated the highest total sales of ₹3.9 lakh.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹7.94 lakh.
- Delhi recorded the highest city-level sales at ₹13.14 lakh.
- Price showed a strong positive relationship with sales (0.77).
- Quantity showed a weak positive relationship with sales (0.28).

## 📈 Key Visualizations

The project includes visualizations for:

## 🎯 Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Python-based data analytics techniques to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. It provides practical experience in data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and interpreting business performance.
